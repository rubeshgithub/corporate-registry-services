Alberta Annual Return Filing — {{price:annual-return-ab}} + GST, all-in (including the government fee). Filed electronically with the Alberta Corporate Registry within 1 business day. No registry office visit, no CORES account, no forms to print.

Every Alberta corporation has to file its annual return with the Alberta Corporate Registry every year, in its anniversary month, to stay in good standing. Every corporation — even one that is not operating — must file, and the filing goes through an authorized service provider because Alberta has no public portal. Search your corporation above: we pull your registry record, show your anniversary date, and file the return for you.

## When is my Alberta annual return due?

In your anniversary month. Your annual-return year ends on the last day of the month you were incorporated in, Corporate Registry mails a reminder to your registered office one month before the anniversary, and the return must be filed within a month of that year-end. Miss it and the corporation drops out of good standing, receives a dissolution warning, and is eventually struck — after which the only way to carry on business is a [corporate revival](/order/revival), which costs far more than the return.

## What do I need to file?

Your corporation's legal name and Corporate Access Number (we pull both when you search), a current email address for the corporation, and the names, addresses and percentage of voting shares of your largest shareholders. If directors or the registered office changed during the year, those are separate notices of change — we ask when you order and can file them with the return.
