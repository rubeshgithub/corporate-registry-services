Nova Scotia corporations must renew their registration with the [Registry of Joint Stock Companies](/articles/registry-of-joint-stock-companies-nova-scotia) every year — the RJSC officially calls it the **annual renewal**, and it's due in the **anniversary month** of your incorporation or registration, one of the tightest windows in Canada. Most people call it the annual return; either way, it's the filing that keeps your corporation in good standing.

## When Is the Nova Scotia Annual Return Due?

Your renewal is due **in the anniversary month that your business originally incorporated or registered** — if your corporation was incorporated on September 5, your renewal is due by the end of September each year. The RJSC sends a renewal notice about a month before, but the obligation is yours whether or not the notice arrives.

## Who Must File?

All corporations registered with the provincial registry, including extra-provincial corporations carrying on business in Nova Scotia.

## What Information Is Required?

- Full legal name and Nova Scotia Registry Number
- Registered office address in Nova Scotia
- Names and addresses of current directors and officers
- Principal business activities

## How to File

Nova Scotia annual renewals are filed online through the **RJSC Connect** portal — see the registry's [renewal page](https://www.novascotia.ca/renew-business-or-non-profit-registration-registry-joint-stock-companies) — or by mail or in person at Access Nova Scotia using the Renewal Notice Form (allow 1–2 weeks for processing).

**Steps:**
1. Sign in or create an account in RJSC Connect
2. Search for your corporation
3. File the renewal and update corporation information
4. Pay the government fee
5. Receive confirmation

## Government Fee

The renewal fee for a Nova Scotia **limited company is $118.35**. Renewal fees vary by legal structure — business names, partnerships, societies and co-operatives each have their own rates on the registry's fee schedule.

## Consequences of Non-Compliance

Given the tight anniversary-month window, a missed renewal puts your corporation out of good standing, and a registration left unrenewed can be struck from the registry. Reinstatement requires a formal application, additional fees, and processing time.
