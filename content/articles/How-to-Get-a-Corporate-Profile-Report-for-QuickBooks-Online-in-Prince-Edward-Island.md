## Why You Need a Corporate Profile Report for QuickBooks Online

QuickBooks Online requires a Corporate Profile Report to verify your business and comply with regulations like FINTRAC or Intuit's requirements for payroll services. The PEI Corporate Profile Report provides the current public record from the Prince Edward Island Corporate Registry, making it the ideal document for QuickBooks verification.

## What's Included in a PEI Corporate Profile Report?

- Entity Name (and any secondary or former names)
- Business Type (Trade Name, Incorporated Company, Limited Partnership, Sole Proprietor, Cooperative, Non-profit, etc.)
- Legal status (Active, Inactive, Expired, Discontinued, Dissolved, etc.)
- Business Number and Registration Number
- Registered office address
- Owner (or directors / shareholders, depending on entity type)
- Nature of business
- Registration Date, Renewal Date, Expiry Date
- Extra-provincial registration info (where applicable)
