Manitoba corporate searches run through the **Companies Office**, part of Entrepreneurship Manitoba — the provincial registry for every Manitoba corporation, business name, trade name and limited partnership. Its online database, **Companies Online**, is free to search, but you need to create a (free) user account before you can run a name or number lookup. This guide covers where to search, what a search result actually shows you, what the registry's certificates and copies cost, and how to search Manitoba companies without creating an account at all.

## Where Do You Search the Manitoba Companies Office?

The Companies Office's search database is [Companies Online](https://companiesonline.gov.mb.ca/), the Government of Manitoba portal used for both searching the registry and filing with it. According to the Companies Office, the public can search the registry to find out who is doing business under a trade name, where a business is located, and the list of a corporation's officers and directors.

Two things to know before you start:

- **An account is required.** Companies Online needs a user name and password even for basic searching. Creating a "Regular Account" is free, and once you're in, the **Search by Name/Number** function covers business names, corporations and trade names.
- **Searching is free of charge.** The Companies Office's own name-reservation guidance confirms the Companies Online database search carries no fee — you only pay when you order documents or file something.

That account step is the main difference from provinces like Nova Scotia, where the public search needs no login. If you'd rather skip the registration, there are two no-account routes covered below.

## Is a Manitoba Companies Office Search Free?

Yes — the database search itself is free once you have a Companies Online account. What costs money is turning a search hit into an official document:

| Companies Office product | Government fee |
|---|---|
| Companies Online database search | Free (account required) |
| Certificate of Status | $40 |
| Certificate of Search | $40 |
| Photocopy of Articles | $20 |
| Certified copy of Articles | $40 |
| Photocopy of other corporate documents | $15 |
| Certified copy of other corporate documents | $20 |

Fees are from the Companies Office's Request for Certificates or Copies form (checked October 2026). Expedited service is available at **double the fee** and is processed within 5 business days; regular paper copy requests are worked through in date order — the Companies Office publishes its current [processing dates](https://companiesoffice.gov.mb.ca/processing.html) so you can see the backlog before you order.

## What Does a Manitoba Search Result Show You?

A Companies Online lookup is the fast way to answer three questions:

- **Does the entity exist?** An exact-name match against registered corporations, business names and trade names tells you whether the company or operating name is actually on the Manitoba register.
- **Who is behind it?** The registry records the officers and directors of a corporation, and who is doing business under a registered trade name — the detail you need before signing a contract, chasing an invoice or vetting a supplier.
- **Is it in good standing?** The record shows whether a corporation is active or has been dissolved or cancelled. A Manitoba corporation that stops filing its annual returns is eventually dissolved for non-filing — so a search is the quickest way to spot a company that has quietly fallen off the register. (If it's your own corporation that's behind, see [how to file your Manitoba annual return](/articles/how-to-file-your-annual-return-in-manitoba) or go straight to the [Manitoba annual return filing service](/annual-return/manitoba-corporate-annual-return) before a bank or buyer runs this same search on you.)

For formal proof — the kind a bank, lawyer or landlord will accept — the search screen isn't enough: you'll want the **Certificate of Status** ($40 government fee, available online), which certifies the corporation exists and its standing on the date of issue. A practical pre-payment check takes five minutes:

1. **Search the name** (Companies Online, or the no-account routes above). No match for the exact name on the invoice is a red flag — trade names must be registered in Manitoba.
