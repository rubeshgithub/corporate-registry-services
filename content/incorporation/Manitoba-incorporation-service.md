## Manitoba incorporation requirements

Under The Corporations Act (Manitoba):

- **Articles of Incorporation** — the founding document specifying name, share structure, and any special provisions.
- **Notice of Directors** — names and addresses of the initial board.
- **Notice of Registered Office** — physical Manitoba address (not a PO box).
- **NUANS report or MB name search** — for named corporations. Numbered corporations skip this.
- **At least one director** — must be 18+. **At least 25% must be Canadian residents.**
- **Filing through the Companies Office Manitoba** — CRS handles this on your behalf; no Companies Office Online account required on your end.
