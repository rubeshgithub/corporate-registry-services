## New Brunswick incorporation requirements

Under the Business Corporations Act (New Brunswick):

- **Articles of Incorporation** — the founding document specifying name, share structure, and any special provisions. Filed in English, French, or both.
- **Notice of Directors** — names and addresses of the initial board.
- **Notice of Registered Office** — physical New Brunswick address (not a PO box).
- **NUANS report or NB name search** — for named corporations. Numbered corporations skip this.
- **At least one director** — must be 18+. **At least 25% must be Canadian residents.**
- **Filing through Service New Brunswick** — CRS handles this on your behalf; no SNB account required.

## What happens after you incorporate

Every New Brunswick corporation has ongoing statutory obligations. In your first year:

- **Annual return** — due by February 1 each year OR within 30 days of your anniversary date, whichever comes first.
