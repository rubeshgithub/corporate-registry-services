## Why incorporate in Newfoundland & Labrador?

**Alignment with offshore energy and seafood.** Newfoundland's economy is anchored in offshore oil and gas (Hibernia, Terra Nova, Hebron), seafood processing, minerals (Voisey's Bay nickel), and hydroelectric generation. Provincial incorporation aligns with the NL regulatory environment and the specific licensing requirements for offshore operations.

**Provincial support for resource and technology sectors.** NL runs several sector-specific incentives targeting resource extraction, ocean technology, and cleantech that generally favour provincially incorporated businesses.

**Straightforward corporations statute.** The Corporations Act (Newfoundland and Labrador) is close in structure to the federal CBCA — Canadian deal counsel and Canadian VC investors are comfortable with NL corporations.

**Atlantic Canada operations stay simple.** For businesses operating primarily in Newfoundland and Labrador or across Atlantic Canada, provincial incorporation avoids the extra-provincial registration burden a federal corporation would carry.

## Newfoundland & Labrador incorporation requirements

Under the Corporations Act (Newfoundland and Labrador):

- **Articles of Incorporation** — the founding document specifying name, share structure, and any special provisions.
- **Notice of Directors** — names and addresses of the initial board.
- **Notice of Registered Office** — physical Newfoundland and Labrador address (not a PO box).
- **NUANS report or NL name search** — for named corporations. Numbered corporations skip this.
- **At least one director** — must be 18+. **At least 25% must be Canadian residents.**
- **Filing through the Registry of Companies** — accessed via Digital Government NL. CRS handles this on your behalf; no provincial portal account required on your end.
