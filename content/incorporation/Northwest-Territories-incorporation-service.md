## Why incorporate in the Northwest Territories?

**Alignment with diamond mining and resource extraction.** The NT economy is anchored in diamond mining (Diavik, Ekati, Gahcho Kué), gold and rare-earth exploration, oil and gas, and increasingly renewable energy. Territorial incorporation aligns with the NT regulatory environment and the licensing regimes specific to northern resource operations.

**Northern logistics and community operations.** Businesses providing logistics, air travel, healthcare, education, and other services to northern communities benefit from provincial-equivalent territorial incorporation — the regulatory alignment is closer and government-procurement processes are territory-specific.

**Straightforward corporations statute.** The Business Corporations Act (Northwest Territories) is close in structure to the federal CBCA — Canadian deal counsel and Canadian investors are comfortable with NT corporations. Standard Articles of Incorporation defaults are conventional and well-understood.

**Direct filing through the NT Corporate Registry.** The NT Corporate Registry, under the Department of Justice, is the single-source registry for all NT filings. CRS files directly on your behalf.

## Northwest Territories incorporation requirements

Under the Business Corporations Act (Northwest Territories):

- **Articles of Incorporation** — the founding document specifying name, share structure, and any special provisions.
- **Notice of Directors** — names and addresses of the initial board.
- **Notice of Registered Office** — physical NT address (not a PO box).
- **NUANS report or NT name search** — for named corporations. Numbered corporations skip this.
- **At least one director** — must be 18+. Specific residency rules should be confirmed with a specialist for your board composition.
- **Filing through the NT Corporate Registry** — CRS handles this on your behalf; no territorial portal account required on your end.
