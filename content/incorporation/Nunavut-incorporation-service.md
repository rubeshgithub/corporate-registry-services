## Nunavut incorporation requirements

Under the Business Corporations Act (Nunavut):

- **Articles of Incorporation** — the founding document specifying name, share structure, and any special provisions.
- **Notice of Directors** — names and addresses of the initial board.
- **Notice of Registered Office** — physical Nunavut address (not a PO box).
- **NUANS report or Nunavut name search** — for named corporations. Numbered corporations skip this.
- **At least one director** — must be 18+. Specific residency rules should be confirmed with a specialist for your board composition.
- **Filing through the Nunavut Corporate Registry** — CRS handles this on your behalf, remotely.
