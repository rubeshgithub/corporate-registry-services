**Atlantic Canada operations stay simple.** For businesses operating primarily in PEI or across Atlantic Canada, provincial incorporation avoids the extra-provincial registration burden a federal corporation would carry.

## PEI incorporation requirements

Under the Business Corporations Act (Prince Edward Island):

- **Articles of Incorporation** — the founding document specifying name, share structure, and any special provisions.
- **Notice of Directors** — names and addresses of the initial board.
- **Notice of Registered Office** — physical PEI address (not a PO box).
- **NUANS report or PEI name search** — for named corporations. Numbered corporations skip this.
- **At least one director** — must be 18+. **At least 25% must be Canadian residents.**
- **Filing through the PEI Corporate Registry** — CRS handles this on your behalf; no provincial portal account required on your end.
