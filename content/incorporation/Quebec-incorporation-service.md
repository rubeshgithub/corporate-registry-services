Quebec is one of the most distinctive Canadian jurisdictions to incorporate in — not because the mechanics are hard, but because the Charter of the French Language and Quebec's own regulatory framework create requirements that don't exist elsewhere. Corporations are formed under the QBCA (Loi sur les sociétés par actions) and filed with the Registraire des entreprises du Québec (REQ). Every Quebec corporation gets a NEQ (Numéro d'entreprise du Québec) as its provincial identifier. ## Why incorporate in Quebec?

**No Canadian resident director requirement.** Quebec's QBCA has never imposed a residency rule on directors — every director just needs to be 18+. Combined with BC, Alberta, and (as of 2023) Ontario and Nova Scotia, Quebec is one of the five Canadian jurisdictions genuinely open to international founders without appointing a token Canadian director for compliance.

**Access to Canada's second-largest business market.** Quebec represents roughly 19% of Canadian GDP, and Montreal is a major hub for tech, biotech, aerospace, and the AI/ML industry. If your customers, employees, or investors are primarily in Quebec, provincial incorporation avoids the extra-provincial registration burden a federal corporation would carry.

**Modern QBCA statute with flexible share provisions.** The QBCA (modernised in 2011) is well-suited to holding structures, family-owned businesses, and estate planning. Flexible share-class provisions support control structures and tax planning that other statutes handle less cleanly.

**Fixed calendar deadline for the annual declaration.** Quebec uses a fixed March 31 deadline for the annual declaration (déclaration annuelle) — every Quebec corporation files by the same date. Once you're aware of it, ongoing compliance planning is easier.

## Quebec incorporation requirements

Under the QBCA (Loi sur les sociétés par actions):

- **Articles of Incorporation (Statuts de constitution)** — the founding document specifying name, share structure, and any special provisions. Filed in French (bilingual optional).
- **French-language name compliance** — under the Charter of the French Language, the corporation must have a French version of its name. An English version is optional but common (bilingual name).
- **Notice of Directors (Avis des administrateurs)** — names and addresses of the initial board.
- **Notice of Registered Office (Avis du siège social)** — physical Quebec address (not a PO box).
- **At least one director** — must be 18+. No Canadian residency requirement.
- **NEQ (Numéro d'entreprise du Québec)** — assigned by the REQ on registration; used on all subsequent Quebec filings.
- **REQ filing** — submitted through the Registraire des entreprises portal. CRS handles this on your behalf; no clicSÉQUR authentication required on your end.
