Saskatchewan is one of the most distinctive Canadian jurisdictions from a filing-mechanics perspective. The province outsourced its corporate registry to ISC (Information Services Corporation), a private company operating under provincial contract, and every corporate filing — incorporation, annual return, name change, share issuance — flows through the ISC Online portal. The upside for a filer: the process is well-run and consistently responsive. The downside if you're going DIY: you need an ISC Online account, which CRS handles for you when you order through us.

## Why incorporate in Saskatchewan?

**Alignment with agriculture, mining, and energy.** Saskatchewan's economy is anchored in agri-food, potash, uranium, oil and gas, and increasingly clean energy. Provincial incorporation aligns you with the SK regulatory environment and provincial programs. For a business primarily operating within the province, SBCA incorporation avoids the extra-provincial registration burden a federal corporation would carry in each province where it operates.

**Straightforward filing through ISC.** ISC's Online portal is reliably fast, and CRS files directly on your behalf — no ISC Online account required on your end. Once names are cleared, the certificate typically issues within a business day.

**Modern Business Corporations Act.** Saskatchewan's SBCA is close to the federal CBCA in structure, so cross-border deal counsel and Canadian VC investors are comfortable with SK corporations. The standard Articles of Incorporation defaults are conventional and well-understood.

**Provincial support programs.** Saskatchewan runs several sector-specific programs (agricultural investment tax credits, mineral exploration incentives, small business rebates) that generally favour provincially incorporated businesses.

## Saskatchewan incorporation requirements

Under The Business Corporations Act (Saskatchewan):

- **Articles of Incorporation** — the founding document specifying name, share structure, and any special provisions.
- **Notice of Directors** — names and addresses of the initial board.
- **Notice of Registered Office** — physical Saskatchewan address (not a PO box).
- **NUANS report or SK name search** — for named corporations. Numbered corporations skip this entirely.
- **At least one director** — must be 18+. **At least 25% must be Canadian residents** (for a 1–3 director board, at least one Canadian resident).
- **Filing through ISC Online** — accessible through Certified Registry Agents (like CRS) or by setting up your own ISC Online account.
