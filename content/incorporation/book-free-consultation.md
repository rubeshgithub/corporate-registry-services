# Book Your Free Incorporation Consultation

Starting a Canadian corporation is a series of small, unforgiving decisions: federal or provincial, named or numbered, how many directors and where they live, one class of shares or several, where the registered office sits. In one free consultation, a CRS incorporation specialist walks through all of it with you — federal or any of Canada's 13 provinces and territories.

Fill in the form on the page and we come to the call prepared: your jurisdiction fit assessed, your three proposed names pre-screened, your directors checked against residency rules, and a step-by-step filing plan for your specific situation.

## What you get in the free consultation

A 30-minute call with a specialist who confirms whether federal (CBCA) or provincial incorporation fits your operations and expansion plans, reviews your proposed names against NUANS or your provincial registry, verifies your director list against any applicable residency rules (federal, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, and others), advises on named vs. numbered, and leaves you with a written checklist of every form, fee, and filing that follows — including the annual return that keeps the corporation in good standing.

There is no obligation. If you only need the checklist, it's yours.
