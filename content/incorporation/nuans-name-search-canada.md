# Free NUANS Name Search: Instant Availability Check for Canadian Corporations

Check how many Canadian corporations already use a similar name — free, instant, across federal + BC + Alberta live registries. A courtesy pre-check to help you decide whether to invest in the official NUANS report before ordering it.

## What is a NUANS report?

**NUANS** stands for *Newly Updated Automated Name Search* — the federal name-search system operated by Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED). It's the authoritative Canadian corporate name search, required by Corporations Canada for federal (CBCA) incorporation, cross-provincial extra-provincial registration, and most corporate name changes. Provincial registrars in Alberta, Nova Scotia, Manitoba, New Brunswick, Newfoundland, and Yukon also require or accept a NUANS.

## What the free check gets you

A traffic-light indicator (strong / moderate / weak), the list of similar corporations we found, and a direct path to order the official NUANS report ($79 all-in + GST, delivered within one business hour) — which registrars require to accept your incorporation filing. Valid for 90 days from issue.
