# Medical Corporation in Manitoba: The CPSM Permit Guide

Manitoba calls it a **medical corporation** rather than a medical professional corporation, and the distinction is more than vocabulary — it signals a structure tied directly to your personal licence. Under *The Regulated Health Professions Act* (RHPA), a physician who wants to practise through a corporation needs **two** things from the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Manitoba: a valid **certificate of practice** for themselves, and a **medical corporation permit** for the corporation.

That coupling is the defining feature of the Manitoba regime. The corporation's permit is renewed alongside your own certificate of practice each year, and if your personal standing lapses, the corporation's authority goes with it.

## How to set up a medical corporation in Manitoba

1. **Confirm you hold a valid certificate of practice.** CPSM's Council Policy on Certificates of Practice is explicit: practising registrants who hold a valid certificate of practice may incorporate and practise through the corporation. Without it, there is nothing to build on.
2. **Get legal and tax advice** on the share structure before anything is filed.
3. **Choose a compliant name** and clear it with CPSM before incorporating, so the corporation you register is one the College will permit.
4. **Incorporate at the Manitoba Companies Office**, with articles carrying the restrictions the RHPA and Regulation 163/2018 require.
5. **Apply to CPSM for the medical corporation permit** — the corporations unit handles this at medcorps@cpsm.mb.ca.
6. **Renew both together every year.** The medical corporation renewal runs alongside your annual certificate of practice renewal, with a separate fee.

The single most important sequencing point: your certificate of practice is the foundation. A physician whose certificate lapses does not simply lose a personal credential — the corporation's authority to practise medicine depends on it.

## Step 1: Incorporate at the Manitoba Companies Office

The corporation itself is created through the Manitoba Companies Office. The articles must carry the restrictions the RHPA and Regulation 163/2018 impose on a medical corporation, and the name must be one CPSM will accept.

Because CPSM has a statutory obligation to notify the Companies Branch when a medical corporation falls out of compliance, the registry record and the College record are actively linked in Manitoba — this is not a case where the registry side can quietly drift. Keeping the corporation current at the Companies Office is part of keeping the permit.

## Step 2: Get your CPSM medical corporation permit

Subsection 60(1) of the RHPA sets out the requirements for the issuance and renewal of a medical corporation permit, with the detail filled in by Regulation 163/2018 and CPSM's bylaws. Applications and questions go to the College's corporations unit at medcorps@cpsm.mb.ca or 204-774-4344.

Section 65 of the Act gives CPSM the power to suspend or cancel a medical corporation permit, and the College's Affairs of the College Bylaw contemplates non-renewal where the requirements are not met. A permit is not a one-time clearance — it is a standing condition the corporation has to keep satisfying.

## Shareholders, directors and restrictions

The share, director and officer requirements for a Manitoba medical corporation are set by the RHPA and Regulation 163/2018 rather than by general corporate law, and they are tied to who holds a certificate of practice. Because these provisions are regulatory and can be amended, confirm the current requirements with your lawyer before finalising the structure — particularly if you are contemplating family shareholders or any arrangement that affects voting control.

What does not change is the underlying principle: incorporating does not separate you from your professional obligations. The corporation practises medicine through you, on the strength of your certificate of practice.

## Keeping it alive: annual renewals

**CPSM track.** The medical corporation permit renews annually, in step with your own certificate of practice renewal. CPSM's renewal guidance lists the **medical corporation renewal fee at $241** on the 2025 schedule, set by the College's Fee Bylaw — check the current bylaw before you pay, because the figure is updated.

The practical trap is treating this as one renewal. It is two: your certificate of practice, and the corporation's permit, each with its own fee. Completing your personal renewal does not renew the corporation.

**Registry track.** The corporation must also stay in good standing at the Manitoba Companies Office. Given CPSM's duty to notify the Companies Branch of non-compliance, the two records are linked in a way that makes drift on either side visible to the other.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I set up a medical corporation in Manitoba?

Confirm you hold a valid certificate of practice, clear a compliant name with CPSM, incorporate at the Manitoba Companies Office with articles meeting the RHPA and Regulation 163/2018 requirements, then apply to CPSM for the medical corporation permit through medcorps@cpsm.mb.ca. Both renew annually.

### Do I need a certificate of practice to incorporate in Manitoba?

Yes. CPSM policy is that practising registrants who hold a valid certificate of practice may incorporate their medicine practice and practise through the corporation. The corporation's permit sits on top of your personal certificate, not beside it.

### How much is the CPSM medical corporation renewal?

The medical corporation renewal fee is listed at $241 on CPSM's 2025 renewal schedule, set by the College's Fee Bylaw. Because the bylaw is updated, confirm the current amount before paying — and note this is separate from your own certificate of practice fee.

### What happens if my medical corporation stops complying?

CPSM is required to notify the Companies Branch when a medical corporation no longer complies with the RHPA, and section 65 of the Act allows the College to suspend or cancel the permit. Non-compliance therefore shows up on both the College and the registry side.

### Is a Manitoba medical corporation the same as a medical professional corporation elsewhere?

Functionally yes, but the vocabulary and the mechanics differ. Manitoba uses "medical corporation" and ties the permit to your certificate of practice under the RHPA, where Ontario issues a certificate of authorization and Alberta issues a permit after endorsing your articles.
