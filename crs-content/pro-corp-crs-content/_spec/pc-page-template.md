## Core editorial frame (every page)
A professional corporation is a TWO-STEP product: (1) incorporate at the corporate registry AND (2) obtain the regulator's permit/authorization — then renew BOTH annually. Always name the regulator, its exact document (permit / certificate of authorization / registration), and its forms. Not tax advice: describe tax angles factually ("small business deduction", "tax deferral") and defer specifics to the reader's accountant.

## Verified facts you may use without re-verifying (from live SERP/regulator research 2026-07-17)
- Ontario physicians: CPSO **certificate of authorization** — $400 application, $175 annual renewal, June 1 renewal deadline; naming rules are strict (must follow required format exactly); O. Reg. 665/05 (Health Profession Corporations) under the OBCA.
- Alberta physicians: CPSA **professional corporation permit** — Professional Corporation Application Form + Professional Corporation Information Form (PCIF); CPSA Bylaws govern registration and annual renewal; CPSA Professional Corporations Guidelines updated May 2025.
- BC: the **Health Professions and Occupations Act (HPOA)** replaced the Health Professions Act; CPSBC issues **health profession corporation permits**; articles must comply with HPOA and college bylaws. Cite HPOA — many competitor posts still cite the repealed HPA.
- Lawyers: Law Society of Alberta — register the PC then apply for/renew a **permit to practise law as a PC**; Law Society of Ontario — **certificate of authorization** for lawyer PCs.
- Accountants: CPA Alberta — Professional Corporation Application Procedures; CPA Ontario — professional corporation **registration**.
- Pharmacists (Ontario): Ontario College of Pharmacists **certificate of authorization**; must incorporate with the Ministry of Public and Business Service Delivery first.
- Dentists (Ontario): RCDSO certificate of authorization (verify current fee/process on rcdso.org before stating specifics).
Re-verify anything beyond these with WebSearch/WebFetch on the regulator's own site before stating it.
