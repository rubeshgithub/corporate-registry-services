# SEO Keyword Strategy — Cluster 1: Not-for-Profit Incorporation (Canada)

**Date:** 2026-07-17 · **Site:** bizfile.ca · **Geography:** Canada (EN) · **Goal:** organic traffic → free-consultation bookings

Methodology note: keyword selection is based on live SERP review of government and competitor pages during research, established Canadian search-demand patterns, and on-page evidence from ranking pages. Difficulty and volume are expert assessments (Low/Med/High), not tool-exported metrics — validate in Google Search Console / a rank tracker after launch.

## Cluster architecture

Pillar: `/not-for-profit/` targeting **"how to start a not-for-profit in Canada"** (High volume, Medium-High difficulty, informational). Fourteen jurisdiction pages capture the high-intent long tail, where difficulty drops sharply because competitors (LawDepot, Ownr, law-firm blogs) mostly cover only ON/BC/AB/Federal. The territories and Atlantic pages are near-uncontested.

## High-value secondary & long-tail keywords (mapped in page frontmatter)

- Cost/fee queries — "cost to register a nonprofit in {province}", "nonprofit incorporation fee Canada". Strong commercial intent; answered in every Key Facts table and an FAQ (featured-snippet format).
- Requirement queries — "how many board members does a nonprofit need in Canada/{province}", "nonprofit board requirements {province}", "minimum directors ONCA". Answered as 40–70-word FAQ snippets targeting PAA boxes.
- Comparison queries — "not-for-profit vs charity Canada", "federal vs provincial incorporation nonprofit", "society vs non-profit company Alberta", "member-funded society BC". Covered in dedicated sections; candidates for future standalone posts.
- Act-name navigational queries — "Societies Act Alberta", "ONCA", "CNCA", "Non-profit Corporations Act Saskatchewan 2022". Included in H1s/first paragraphs; act-name queries convert well because searchers are already in-process.
- Process queries — "NUANS name search nonprofit", "nonprofit annual return {province}", "how long does nonprofit incorporation take". Covered in step-by-step and compliance sections.

## Quick wins (prioritize for launch + internal links from homepage)

1. All 6 society-act jurisdictions (NS, YT, NWT, NU + AB, BC) — "societies act" queries have clear intent, thin competition outside government pages, and our pages out-detail the gov pages on process.
2. Saskatchewan — the 2022 act is new; most third-party content still cites the 1995 act. Freshness advantage.
3. PEI/NB/NL — virtually no commercial competitors publish letters-patent guides; expect fast top-3 rankings.
4. Consultation page — ranks via internal links; optimize for conversion, not search.

## SERP-feature strategy

- FAQ sections use question-phrased H3s with direct 40–70-word answers → FAQPage schema (JSON-LD) for rich results and AI-answer (GEO) citation.
- Key Facts tables are extraction-friendly for AI Overviews — keep the label/value format.
- Every page names its act + section numbers where verified — authoritativeness signal that generic incorporation-service pages lack.

## On-page checklist (applies to all pages — already implemented in the .md files)

Primary keyword in title tag (≤60 chars), meta description (≤155 chars), H1, first 100 words, and one H2. One internal link to pillar, sibling grants page, and consultation page minimum. External links only to official government sources (trust signal). `lastUpdated` surfaced on-page ("Reviewed July 2026") and refreshed at each fee/act change — fee accuracy is this cluster's core E-E-A-T asset.

## Measurement

Track in GSC: impressions/clicks per jurisdiction page, PAA/FAQ rich-result wins, and consultation-page assisted conversions. Re-run SERP checks quarterly; provincial fees and intake rules change and stale figures kill rankings and trust.
