## What is a digital minute book?

A digital minute book (also called a **virtual minute book**, online minute book, or electronic minute book) is your corporation's official record book maintained in electronic form: articles of incorporation, by-laws, director and shareholder resolutions, share certificates, and all statutory registers — organized, searchable, and shareable with your accountant, banker, or lawyer in seconds.

Canadian corporate law (the CBCA and every provincial equivalent) fully permits electronic records, as long as they're organized, accessible, and producible for inspection. A digital minute book satisfies the same legal requirement as a physical one — without the binder that never gets updated.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a digital minute book legal in Canada?

Yes. The Canada Business Corporations Act and all provincial corporate statutes permit corporate records to be kept electronically, provided they are organized, accessible, and can be produced in readable form for inspection at the registered office. A digital minute book satisfies the same requirement as a paper binder.

### How fast will I receive my minute book?

Standard packages for corporations up to two years old are typically delivered within two business days. Established and Legacy packages requiring government document retrieval take longer depending on the registry — we confirm the timeline when you order.

### I incorporated online and never got a minute book. Is that a problem?

Yes — incorporation services often deliver articles only, leaving the corporation without by-laws, organizational resolutions, registers, or share certificates. Your corporation is legally required to maintain these records. Our Standard package ($299 all-in + GST) creates the complete minute book from your incorporation documents.

### What's the difference between a minute book and an annual return?

An annual return is a yearly government filing that keeps your corporation in good standing on the public registry. A minute book is your internal record of resolutions, registers, and share documents. Both are legally required — we handle annual returns for $99 all-in + GST and minute books from $299.
